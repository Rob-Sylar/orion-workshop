# Mission: Car Valeting Tool

- **Date:** 2026-11-22
- **Status:** ✓ Complete
- **Asked:** Car valeting tool: price calculator, job log, earnings.

## What was built
Three tabs. Quote: choose service, car size and add-ons for an instant price, then add it as a job. Jobs: weekly, monthly and unpaid totals, with mark-paid and delete. My prices: edit every price (starter prices are guesses).

## How to use
Set your real prices in 'My prices' first, then quote and log jobs. Only first names are stored, never addresses or numbers.
