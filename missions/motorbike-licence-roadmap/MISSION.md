# Mission: Motorbike Licence Roadmap

- **Date:** 2026-11-22
- **Status:** ✓ Complete
- **Asked:** Motorbike licence roadmap: CBT to full licence with checklists, costs and a lesson log.

## What was built
8 expandable stages (provisional, CBT, theory, route choice, DAS training, Module 1, Module 2, after passing), each with what to expect and a checklist. Overall progress bar. Lesson log with date, what you did and notes.

## How to use
Tap a stage to open it, tick things off as you go, and log each lesson.
