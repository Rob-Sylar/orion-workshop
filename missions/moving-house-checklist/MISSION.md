# Mission: Moving House Checklist

- **Date:** 2026-11-22
- **Status:** ✓ Complete
- **Asked:** Moving house checklist from offer to first month, plus who to tell.

## What was built
7 stages of moving tasks plus a 'who to tell' list (21 organisations), each with progress counts and an overall bar.

## How to use
Open a stage, tick as you go. Progress saves on the device.
