# Mission: Shift Handover Checklist

- **Date:** 2026-11-22
- **Status:** ✓ Complete
- **Asked:** Shift handover checklist with tick boxes and a notes box (generic, no store details).

## What was built
From/to names, date and shift. 6 sections (tills, deliveries/stock, shop floor, people, H&S, handover) with ticks and progress, a notes box, and a 'Copy handover' button that makes a ✅/⬜ summary ready for WhatsApp. 'Start a new shift' clears it.

## How to use
Tick through, add notes, tap Copy, paste into the team chat.
