# Mission: Telly Tracker

- **Date:** 2026-11-22
- **Status:** ✓ Complete
- **Asked:** Telly tracker: shows watching/want to watch with episode ticks.

## What was built
Add shows with seasons and episodes per season. Filter by watching, want or finished. Tap a show to open its episode grid, tick episodes, tick whole seasons, edit episode counts, add seasons. Shows 'Next up' and % progress, and auto-moves to finished at 100%.

## How to use
Add a show, tap it to open, tick episodes as you watch.
